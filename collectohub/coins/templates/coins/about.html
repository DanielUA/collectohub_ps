About CollectoHub

Welcome to CollectoHub!

CollectoHub is a unique platform designed for coin enthusiasts and collectors to exchange collectible coins. We offer a safe and convenient way to trade coins, allowing you to enrich your collection with new and rare pieces.

My Passion for Coin Collecting

I have been passionate about coin collecting since childhood, long before the internet made it easy to connect with other collectors. Back then, people exchanged coins in person, and this inspired me to recreate that experience in a modern way through this platform. The goal of CollectoHub is to revive the traditional coin-swapping method in a safe and convenient online environment.

Why Use CollectoHub?

Every coin collector accumulates duplicate coins or coins that do not fit into their collection. These coins can be difficult to sell due to their low value, and listing them individually on marketplaces can take months before they even get noticed. CollectoHub was created to solve this problem by providing an easy and efficient way to trade coins.

You don’t need to spend time photographing your coins, creating listings, or waiting for buyers. Instead, you simply send your coins to us, and we take care of the rest. Our platform allows you to exchange one coin for multiple coins, meaning if you find a coin that interests you, you can propose a flexible trade—offering several coins in return rather than just one.

How Does It Work?

  • Registration:

    Start by registering on our platform. This will give you access to your personal cabinet, where you can manage your coins, track exchanges, and interact with other members.

  • Sending Your Coins:

    Once registered, send the coins you wish to trade to me at the provided mailing address. I will photograph and catalog them before uploading them to your personal cabinet, where you can manage your offers and view available trading options.

  • Photography and Listing:

    Upon receiving your coins, I will carefully photograph and describe each one. These images and descriptions will be available in your account for easy management.

  • Trading Process:

    You can trade coins with other members of the platform, including myself. Our system allows flexible trading—you can offer one coin in exchange for multiple coins or negotiate different trade deals based on what best suits your collection.

  • Receiving Your Traded Coins:

    Once you finalize your trades, I will send your updated collection back to you.

Service Fees

Since CollectoHub is a new project, there is currently no fee for trading coins—only postal expenses apply. The cost of shipping starts at 85 pence for a letter up to 100 grams (approximately 20-30 coins). This limit ensures that I can process and document each coin efficiently.
